# İlkOku SEO & Social Brand Title

Canonical homepage / social identity title:

**İlkOku | Dijital Edebiyat Platformu – İlk cümle, ilk adım**

Use this exact title for the live homepage `<title>`, Open Graph title and Twitter/X title. The global social artwork should visually carry **Dijital Edebiyat Platformu** and **İlk cümle, ilk adım.**

The editorial brand thought **“İlk cümle, ilk okurun, ilk adımın.”** remains the literary slogan used in editorial/footer contexts; it is not replaced by the shorter SEO/social title line.
